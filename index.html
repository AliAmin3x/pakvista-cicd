Why Visit Pakistan?

A country of contrasts, Pakistan offers something for every kind of traveller.

Rich History

Pakistan is home to some of the world's oldest civilisations, including the Indus Valley Civilisation at Mohenjo-daro and Harappa, along with magnificent Mughal-era monuments that rival any in South Asia.

Natural Wonders

Three of the world's fourteen eight-thousanders lie within Pakistan's borders. The Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindu Kush mountain ranges make the country a paradise for trekkers, climbers, and nature lovers.

Vibrant Culture

From the bustling street food scene of Lahore and Karachi to the traditional Chitrali music of the north, Pakistan's cultural tapestry is vivid, warm, and deeply welcoming to visitors from around the world.
